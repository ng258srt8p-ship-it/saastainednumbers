## What Is Canvas?

Canvas is a multi-calculator workspace built directly into saastainednumbers.com. Instead of using one calculator at a time, you can load several calculators onto a single workspace, adjust inputs across all of them, and watch your metrics update in real time — with an aggregate Executive Summary that rolls up the numbers across every calculator on your board.

Think of it as a financial command center for your SaaS business.

[**Try Canvas →**](/canvas)

## Why We Built It

We noticed something about how founders actually use calculators. They don't just want one number — they want to understand relationships. How does a 2% drop in churn affect your LTV:CAC ratio? What happens to your runway when you increase marketing spend by 30%? How does your MRR growth rate compare against your burn multiple?

Answering these questions with individual calculators is possible, but it's slow and error-prone. You'd need to remember outputs from one calculator, manually feed them into another, and keep a running tally of everything. Most people give up and just eyeball it.

Canvas eliminates that friction entirely. Load the calculators you need, adjust the inputs that matter, and see the full picture emerge — live, connected, and aggregate.

## What Makes Canvas Different

### Drag-and-Drop Workspace

The Canvas workspace is fully interactive. Browse the Calculator Catalog on the left sidebar — 75 calculators across 9 categories — and click or drag any calculator onto your workspace. Remove them just as easily. Your workspace persists in your browser, so when you come back, everything is exactly where you left it.

### 10 Curated Templates

Not sure where to start? Canvas ships with 10 pre-built templates designed for common analysis workflows:

- **SaaS Starter Pack** — MRR, churn, LTV, CAC, and gross margin in one view
- **Unit Economics Deep Dive** — LTV, CAC, payback period, margins, and LTV:CAC ratio
- **Growth Efficiency Suite** — Quick ratio, magic number, Rule of 40, burn multiple, and NRR
- **Revenue Metrics** — MRR, ARPU, MRR growth rate, expansion revenue, and net cash flow
- **AI Cost Analyzer** — Compare API costs across Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok
- **Personal Finance Planner** — FIRE, savings rate, emergency fund, investments, and debt payoff
- **Side Hustle Stack** — Freelance rate, affiliate income, newsletter revenue, and more
- **Business Health Check** — Break-even, burn rate, cash runway, employee cost, and ROI
- **Churn & Retention Audit** — Churn, NPS, engagement score, health score, and activation rate
- **Blank Canvas** — Start fresh with an empty workspace

Each template loads a curated set of calculators onto your workspace with sensible defaults, so you can start analyzing immediately.

### Executive Summary

At the top of every Canvas workspace sits the Executive Summary — an aggregate dashboard that rolls up key metrics across all your active calculators. Total MRR, total ARR, average LTV, best LTV:CAC ratio, average churn rate — all computed in real time as you adjust inputs.

This is the view your board wants to see.

### Real-Time Cross-Calculator Visibility

Every calculator on your workspace runs its own engine independently, but the Executive Summary connects them. Adjust a single input on one calculator, and you'll see how it ripples through your aggregate metrics instantly. No manual recalculation. No copy-paste. Just live numbers.

## How to Use Canvas

### Getting Started

1. **Go to [saastainednumbers.com/canvas](/canvas)**
2. **Pick a template** from the Quick Start Templates section, or browse the Calculator Catalog
3. **Click or drag** calculators onto your workspace
4. **Adjust inputs** using the sliders on each calculator widget
5. **Watch the Executive Summary** update in real time as you tweak your numbers

### Building a Custom Analysis

Say you want to understand the relationship between your growth rate, burn multiple, and cash runway. Here's how:

1. Start with a **Blank Canvas** template
2. Add the **MRR Growth Rate** calculator
3. Add the **Burn Multiple** calculator
4. Add the **Cash Runway** calculator
5. Adjust your MRR growth rate and watch how it affects burn multiple and runway calculations

The Executive Summary at the top will show your aggregate revenue, cost, and ratio metrics across all three calculators.

### Comparing Scenarios

Canvas makes scenario analysis trivial. Want to compare your current metrics against an aggressive growth plan?

1. Load your current numbers into the workspace
2. Note the Executive Summary metrics
3. Adjust inputs to model the aggressive scenario
4. Compare the new Executive Summary against your baseline

No spreadsheets. No formulas. Just slide the inputs and see what changes.
