Your go-to-market (GTM) strategy is how you bring your product to customers. But without measurement, you can't know which parts of your GTM engine are working and which are wasting money. GTM metrics connect your marketing, sales, and customer success activities to revenue outcomes.

## The GTM Funnel

Modern GTM funnels are complex, with multiple channels, touchpoints, and conversion paths. The key is measuring each stage:

### 1. Top of Funnel: Awareness

- **Traffic by channel:** Organic, paid, social, referral, direct
- **Share of voice:** Your visibility vs. competitors
- **Content engagement:** Time on page, scroll depth, downloads
- **Lead generation rate:** Visitors who become leads

### 2. Middle of Funnel: Consideration

- **Lead conversion rate:** Percentage of leads that become qualified. Use our [lead conversion rate calculator](/growth-efficiency/lead-conversion-rate-calculator)
- **Demo requests and attendance:** Interest signals
- **Trial starts:** Users who begin a free trial
- **Activation rate:** Users who reach the aha moment. Use our [activation rate calculator](/growth-efficiency/activation-rate-calculator)

### 3. Bottom of Funnel: Conversion

- **Trial to paid conversion rate:** Use our [trial to paid calculator](/revenue/trial-to-paid-calculator)
- **Sales velocity:** Speed from lead to closed deal
- **Win rate:** Percentage of qualified opportunities that close
- **Average contract value (ACV):** Use our [ARPU calculator](/revenue/arpu-calculator)

### 4. Post-Sale: Retention & Expansion

- **Churn rate:** Use our [churn calculator](/churn-retention/churn-calculator)
- **Net Revenue Retention:** Use our [NRR calculator](/revenue/nrr-calculator)
- **Customer health score:** Use our [customer health score calculator](/churn-retention/customer-health-score-calculator)
- **Expansion revenue rate:** Use our [expansion revenue rate calculator](/revenue/expansion-revenue-rate-calculator)

| Segment | GTM Motion | Key Metric |
|---------|------------|------------|
| Self-serve | Product-led, content marketing | Activation rate |
| Transactional | Inbound sales, email | Trial conversion |
| Mid-market | Inside sales, demos | Sales velocity |
| Enterprise | Field sales, partnerships | Win rate |

## Common GTM Metric Mistakes

- **Vanity metrics:** Traffic without conversion is noise
- **Attribution complexity:** Use multi-touch attribution for accurate channel performance
- **Not segmenting by channel:** Aggregate metrics hide channel-specific issues
- **Ignoring time lag:** GTM efforts take 30-90 days to show results
