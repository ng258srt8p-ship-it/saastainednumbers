SaaS company valuation is both art and science. While the final number depends on market conditions, investor demand, and negotiation, the underlying metrics that drive valuation are well understood. If you know which metrics matter, you can take concrete steps to increase your company's worth.

## The Basics of SaaS Valuation

The most common SaaS valuation method is the revenue multiple: your annual recurring revenue (ARR) multiplied by a factor. In 2026, healthy growth stage SaaS companies trade at 10x to 30x ARR, depending on growth rate, retention, and market position.

Key formula:
**Valuation = ARR × Multiple**

## The Metrics That Drive Your Multiple

### 1. Revenue Growth Rate

Growth rate is the single biggest driver of valuation multiple. Companies growing 30%+ YoY command significantly higher multiples than those growing 10-20%.

| Growth Rate (YoY) | Typical Multiple |
|-------------------|------------------|
| 100%+ | 30x - 50x |
| 50-100% | 20x - 30x |
| 30-50% | 12x - 20x |
| 10-30% | 6x - 12x |
| <10% | 3x - 6x |

### 2. Net Revenue Retention (NRR)

NRR above 120% signals that your existing customers are growing faster than churn is reducing revenue. Companies with high NRR command higher multiples because they have a built-in growth engine. Use our [NRR calculator](/revenue/nrr-calculator).

### 3. Gross Margin

Higher gross margin means more of each revenue dollar is available for growth investment. Public SaaS companies with >80% gross margin trade at higher multiples. Use our [gross margin calculator](/revenue/gross-margin-calculator).

### 4. Rule of 40

The Rule of 40 (growth rate + profit margin >= 40%) is the most widely used health metric in SaaS M&A and public market investing. Companies above 40% are considered excellent. Our [Rule of 40 calculator](/growth-efficiency/rule-of-40-calculator) shows where you stand.

### 5. LTV:CAC Ratio

Efficient growth is valued higher than spending growth. An LTV:CAC ratio above 5x indicates efficient growth and justifies a higher multiple. Use our [LTV calculator](/revenue/ltv-calculator) and [CAC calculator](/growth-efficiency/cac-calculator).

## Other Factors Investors Consider

- **Market size:** TAM (Total Addressable Market) affects potential scale
- **Market position:** Category leader vs. challenger
- **Sales efficiency:** Magic Number and payback period. Use our [magic number calculator](/growth-efficiency/magic-number-calculator) and [payback period calculator](/unit-economics/payback-period-calculator)
- **Team quality:** Track record and domain expertise
- **Customer concentration:** Risk from depending on a few large accounts

## How to Improve Your Valuation

- Accelerate growth (even at the expense of profitability for high-growth companies)
- Improve NRR through expansion revenue and churn reduction
- Increase gross margin through infrastructure optimization
- Extend runway to reduce financing risk. Use our [burn rate calculator](/growth-efficiency/burn-rate-calculator)
