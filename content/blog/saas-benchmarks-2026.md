## How to Use These Benchmarks

Benchmarks provide context, not targets. Your specific metrics depend on your stage, business model, and market. Use benchmarks to:

- Identify areas where you're significantly above or below median
- Prioritize improvement efforts
- Prepare for investor conversations
- Set realistic targets for your team
