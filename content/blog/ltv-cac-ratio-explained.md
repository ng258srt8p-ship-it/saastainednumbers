If you could only track one metric to understand the health of your SaaS business, the LTV:CAC ratio would be a strong contender. It captures the fundamental economics of your business model: what it costs to acquire a customer versus how much revenue that customer generates over their lifetime.

## What Is LTV:CAC?

The LTV:CAC ratio compares Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) to Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC). It tells you whether each dollar spent on acquisition generates enough long-term revenue to build a profitable business.

The formula is:

**LTV:CAC = Customer Lifetime Value / Customer Acquisition Cost**

## Why 3:1 Is the Minimum

Industry benchmarks consistently point to 3:1 as the minimum healthy LTV:CAC ratio. Here's why:

- **Below 3:1:** Your unit economics are fragile. Any increase in churn, drop in pricing, or rise in acquisition costs puts you in negative territory
- **3:1 to 5:1:** Healthy range. You're generating good returns on acquisition spend
- **Above 5:1:** You may be under-investing in growth. Consider spending more on acquisition to capture market share

The 3:1 minimum accounts for overhead costs not included in CAC (G&A, R&D, facilities) and provides a buffer for market fluctuations.

## How to Calculate LTV

Customer Lifetime Value depends on three inputs:

**LTV = ARPU × Gross Margin / Churn Rate**

For example, if your ARPU is $100/month, gross margin is 80%, and monthly churn is 5%:

LTV = $100 × 0.80 / 0.05 = $1,600

If your CAC is $400, your LTV:CAC ratio is 4:1, a healthy business.

## How to Improve Your Ratio

### Increase LTV

- **Reduce churn:** Every point of churn reduction significantly increases LTV. Track churn with our [churn calculator](/churn-retention/churn-calculator)
- **Increase ARPU:** Raise prices, encourage upgrades, or add usage-based components
- **Improve gross margin:** Optimize infrastructure costs and delivery efficiency

### Decrease CAC

- **Focus on high-conversion channels:** Double down on organic and referral channels
- **Improve sales efficiency:** Shorten sales cycles and reduce wasted prospecting
- **Product-led growth:** Let virality and word-of-mouth reduce acquisition costs
