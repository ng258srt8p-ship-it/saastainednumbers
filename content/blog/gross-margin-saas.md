Gross margin tells you how much of your revenue is available to fund growth after paying for the direct costs of delivering your service. It's one of the most fundamental measures of business health, and it directly impacts your valuation, fundraising ability, and strategic options.

## What Is Gross Margin?

Gross margin measures revenue minus the direct costs of delivering your product (Cost of Goods Sold, or COGS), expressed as a percentage.

**Gross Margin = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue × 100**

For a SaaS company with $1M in revenue and $250K in hosting and support costs, gross margin is 75%.

## What's Included in COGS?

For SaaS companies, COGS typically includes:

- **Cloud infrastructure:** AWS, GCP, Azure, or other hosting
- **Third-party APIs:** Payment processing, email delivery, SMS
- **Customer support:** Support team salaries and tools
- **Professional services:** Implementation and training costs
- **Content delivery:** CDN, bandwidth, and storage costs

## What Good Gross Margin Looks Like

| Tier | Gross Margin | Examples |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Excellent | 80-90% | Mature cloud SaaS |
| Good | 70-80% | Typical SaaS |
| Average | 60-70% | SaaS with significant services |
| Needs improvement | Below 60% | Infrastructure-heavy or services-heavy |

Public SaaS companies average 75% gross margin. Top-quartile companies achieve 80%+.

## Why Gross Margin Matters

- **Valuation:** Higher gross margin companies command higher multiples
- **Growth capacity:** More margin dollars available for sales and marketing
- **Profitability path:** Higher margins mean reaching profitability faster
- **Pricing flexibility:** Higher margins give you room to compete on price

## How to Improve Gross Margin

### Reduce Infrastructure Costs

- Negotiate cloud provider discounts (reserved instances, committed use)
- Optimize database and compute usage
- Implement cost monitoring and alerting
- Consider multi-cloud or hybrid strategies

### Automate Support

- Implement self-service knowledge bases
- Use chatbots for Tier 1 support
- Build automated onboarding flows
- Create community forums for peer support

### Optimize Delivery

- Improve deployment efficiency
- Reduce API dependency costs
- Streamline professional services delivery
- Build product features that reduce support tickets

### Pricing Adjustments

- Raise prices to improve margin percentage
- Add premium tiers with higher margins
- Bundle services into higher-value packages

## Common Gross Margin Mistakes

- **Not tracking at all:** Many early-stage companies don't calculate gross margin
- **Including G&A costs:** Gross margin should only include direct costs
- **Ignoring professional services:** Services drag down margins; track software and services separately
- **Not improving over time:** Gross margin should increase as you scale
