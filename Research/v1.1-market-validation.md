## Product Concept
A modern web-based **SaaS & Business Operations Calculator Suite** — 40-50 interconnected calculators for SaaS founders, operators, and business professionals. Each calculator targets a specific high-value keyword and provides instant, shareable results.

### Calculator Categories (Tier 1 — Launch)
1. **Revenue Metrics**: MRR, ARR, ARPU, NRR, GRR
2. **Unit Economics**: LTV, CAC, LTV:CAC Ratio, CAC Payback Period
3. **Churn & Retention**: Monthly Churn, Annual Churn, Quick Ratio, Revenue Churn
4. **Growth & Efficiency**: Growth Rate Projector, Rule of 40, Burn Multiple, Magic Number
5. **Cost & Pricing**: SaaS Pricing Optimizer, Burn Rate, Runway, Gross Margin, Break-Even
6. **Marketing ROI**: ROAS, CPM, CPC, CTR, Conversion Rate, AOV
7. **Freelance & Ops**: Freelance Rate, Remote Work Savings, Employee Cost, Revenue Per Employee

## Market Research

### Total Addressable Market
- Global SaaS market: **$312-435B** (2026) growing at **14-21% CAGR** (Gartner, Mordor Intelligence, Technavio)
- SaaS founders and operators are the target demographic — they search for tools to calculate MRR, CAC, LTV, churn, and pricing multiple times per week
- North America accounts for **46-57%** of SaaS market — our primary traffic target for highest RPM

### Demand Validation
- HustleCalcs (54 calculators) reports 1.3M calculations/month with 38% MoM growth — real and growing demand
- OnlineCalcAI (206 calculators × 30 languages) generates ~65K sessions/month, ~$10K/month AdSense
- CalculatorCove (700+ calculators across 14 categories) generates 50-100K sessions/month via pure static HTML + display ads
- SaaSMetricsCalculator (12 SaaS calculators) runs all-free with no monetization — likely agency lead gen
- FoundStep is NOT a calculator competitor — it's a project management tool with a few free side tools
- MetricNexus.ai appears to be offline or domain changed — not a viable competitor

**Gap Identified** (confirmed by live competitor dissection): No existing site combines:
1. SaaS/B2B niche focus with 40+ interconnected calculators
2. Embeddable/shareable widgets (zero competitors do this)
3. Freemium + API + ads revenue mix
4. Deep content (500+ words per calc + schema markup)
5. Developer API access (no competitor offers programmatic access)

## Key Assumptions (Corrected)
1. Starting RPM is $3-8, not $35. $15-25 achievable at 80-100K sessions with premium network
2. Traffic of ~30K sessions/month by month 12 is realistic, not 40K pageviews
3. Freemium conversion rate of 1-2%, not 1-3%
4. MVP is 20 calculators (not 40) + 10 per month thereafter
5. Competition is moderate — HustleCalcs and CalculatorCove are established but have clear gaps
6. **No competitor offers embeddable widgets or API access** — this is our primary differentiator
