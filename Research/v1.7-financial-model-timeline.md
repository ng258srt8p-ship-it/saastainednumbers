## Capital Efficiency

This business is **capital-efficient by design**:
- No server costs (Vercel free tier → CDN static files)
- No paid ads (organic-only for first 12 months)
- No employee costs (solo founder)
- No office/overhead (remote)
- Tool costs < $30/mo for first year

**Every dollar earned is nearly pure profit after ~$30/mo fixed costs.**

## Key Risks to Financial Model

1. **Traffic growth is unrealistic** — The model assumes compounding SEO growth. If the site gets hit by a Google update, the 18-month timeline extends to 24-36 months.
2. **RPM continues declining** — Google's share of publisher ad revenue has been shrinking for years. RPM may be lower than modeled by 2027.
3. **Ad blocker adoption accelerates** — Already at 45-65% for tech audiences. If this hits 70-80%, display revenue becomes negligible.
4. **Freemium requires critical mass** — Subscription revenue doesn't materialize until 1,000+ registered users (month 10-12 at earliest).
5. **Competition floods the niche** — If the model works, others will copy it. First-mover advantage in SEO matters, but the window may be 6-12 months.
