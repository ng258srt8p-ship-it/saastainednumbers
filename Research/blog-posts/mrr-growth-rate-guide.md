## Outline

### H2: What Is MRR Growth Rate?
- Definition: percentage change in MRR period-over-period
- Why it's the single most important leading indicator of SaaS health
- How it differs from traditional revenue growth metrics

### H2: How to Calculate MRR Growth Rate
- Formula: (Current MRR - Previous MRR) / Previous MRR × 100
- Month-over-month vs year-over-year calculations
- Net vs gross MRR growth
- Internal link: [MRR Growth Rate Calculator](/revenue/mrr-growth-rate-calculator)

### H2: What Good MRR Growth Looks Like by Stage
- Seed stage ($0-$1M ARR): 15-20% MoM target
- Series A ($1M-$5M ARR): 10-15% MoM
- Growth stage ($5M-$20M ARR): 5-10% MoM
- Scale stage ($20M+ ARR): 2-5% MoM
- Internal link: [MRR Calculator](/revenue/mrr-calculator)

### H2: Common MRR Growth Mistakes
- Confusing logo growth with revenue growth
- Ignoring seasonality in month-over-month comparisons
- Not segmenting growth by product line or customer cohort
- Celebrating high percentage growth on a tiny base
