## Revenue Model Architecture

### Tier 1: Display Advertising (Primary - 80-85% of revenue)

**Ad Network Progression Plan (Pivot 2026  -  EthicalAds + Mediavine):**
| Stage | Traffic (sessions/mo) | Network | Est. RPM | Est. Monthly Rev |
|-------|----------------------|---------|----------|-----------------|
| 0 (Launch) | 0-1K | No ads (content building) | $0 | $0 |
| 1 (Months 3-6) | 1K-5K | AdSense (EthicalAds requires ~10K sessions) | $3-5 | $3-25 |
| 2 (Months 6-12) | 5K-30K | EthicalAds primary + AdSense fallback | $8-12 blended | $40-360 |
| 3 (Months 12-18) | 30K-60K | Mediavine Journey (1K sessions min, 75% rev share) | $8-15 | $240-900 |
| 4 (Months 18-24) | 60K-100K | Full Mediavine ($5K/yr ad revenue min) | $15-25 | $900-2,500 |
| 5 (Months 24+) | 100K+ | Raptive (100K PV min) | $20-30 | $2,000-3,000 |

**Rationale for EthicalAds over AdSense for mid-stage:**
- **Tech audience fit**: EthicalAds serves developer/SaaS ads  -  relevant to calculator users
- **Higher CPM**: $8-12 vs $3-5 for AdSense  -  2-3× better RPM from day 1
- **Ad blocker friendly**: EthicalAds is allow-listed by many ad blockers (recovery without server-side hacks)
- **Non-intrusive**: Footer/text placements match content-focused UX
- **Fill rate consideration**: EthicalAds may not fill 100%  -  use AdSense as programmatic fallback via passback. At 5K sessions/mo, EthicalAds fill may be ~40-60%; AdSense fills the gap.

**Path to $1,000-2,000/month via ads alone:**
- At $10 RPM (Mediavine Journey, 75% rev share): need 100K-200K sessions/month
- At $20 RPM (full Mediavine, 100% rev share): need 50K-100K sessions/month
- At $12 RPM (EthicalAds sustained): need 80K-170K sessions/month
- With EthicalAds starting at 1K sessions ($8-12 RPM when it fills), ad revenue begins earlier than AdSense-only model
- $1,000/mo target shifts from month 18-20 to month 20-24 without freemium contribution

## Ad Placement Strategy

### Placement Types (Ranked by Revenue vs UX Impact)

| Placement | Est. Revenue Impact | UX Impact | Recommended? |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------|-------------|
| In-content (between results & FAQ) | High | Low | ✅ Primary |
| Below calculator results | Medium | Low | ✅ Always |
| Sidebar (on blog pages) | Medium | Minimal | ✅ Blog only |
| Footer (sticky bar) | Low | Medium | ❌ Avoid |
| Interstitial (full-screen) | High | Very High | ❌ Avoid |
| Above calculator (banner) | Medium | Medium | ⚠️ Test |

### Recommended Layout Per Page Type

**Calculator pages** (primary revenue pages):
1. Below results area (native-style text ad or display unit)
2. Between results section and FAQ section (ethical ad placement)
3. Below FAQ / at page footer (low-impact filler)

**Blog / content pages**:
1. Sidebar ad (if page width allows)
2. In-content ad after 2nd paragraph
3. Below article / related posts

**Embedded calculators** (third-party sites):
- No ads on embeds  -  embeds are a distribution channel, not a revenue channel
- Attribution link back to webcalc.io for referral traffic

### Ad Load Strategy

| Traffic Level | Ad Load | Rationale |
|--------------|---------|-----------|
| 0-1K sessions/mo | 0 ads | Build content, no UX friction |
| 1K-10K | 1 ad per page (below results) | Conservative  -  maintain engagement |
| 10K-50K | 2 ads per page (below results + between sections) | Scale with traffic |
| 50K+ | 3 ads max (including sidebar on blog) | Full optimization without overloading |

**Golden rule**: Never show more than 2 display ads per calculator page. The tool itself is the value prop  -  ads are secondary.

## Updated Assumptions (Ad-Supported Model)

1. Ad revenue alone requires 70-100K+ sessions at $10-15 RPM to hit $1,000+/mo  -  affiliate is additive but not a primary driver

5. No competitor monetizes via API or embeds  -  these are pure uncontested revenue streams
6. **Without freemium, the traffic bar is higher**: $1,000/mo requires ~100K sessions at $10 RPM (vs ~80K with freemium). SEO strategy must be more aggressive.
7. **Ad placement matters more**: Optimal placement (below results, between sections) vs lazy loading and viewability optimization can improve effective RPM by 20-40%
