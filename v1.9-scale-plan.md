# v1.9 — Scale Plan ($5K-$20K/Month)

**Incorporating corrections from v1.x Critical Challenge.** This plan assumes the base case ($1,000-2,000/mo) is achieved by month 18-24, then accelerates to $5K, $10K, and $20K/mo via new products, channels, and team expansion.

---

## Scale Phase Overview

```
Revenue
  ^
$20K ── ★ (Month 36-48: $20K/mo — API licensing + white-label)
  │
$10K ── ★ (Month 30-36: $10K/mo — Premium B2B + scale)
  │
$5K ──── ★ (Month 24-30: $5K/mo — Freemium growth + affiliates)
  │
$2K ──── ★ (Month 18-24: Base case achieved)
  │
$1K ──── ★ (Month 18: Base case floor)
  │
$0 ────────●─────────●─────────●─────────●──►
            M12       M18       M24     M36-48
```

### Revenue Mix at $20K

| Source | Monthly | % | Growth Driver |
|--------|---------|---|---------------|
| Display ads | $5,000 | 25% | 250K sessions × $20 RPM (premium network) |

### What Must Change to Reach $20K

| Change | Investment | Impact |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Full team: 1 dev + 1 designer + 6 content | $12,000/mo | Scale all channels |
| Enterprise sales | Founder time | Direct B2B sales for white-label |
| SaaS metrics data subscription | Custom dev | Companies pay for aggregated calculator data |
| Calculator marketplace | Platform dev | Third-party devs build calculators → revenue share |
| Exit optionality | Legal/financial | Clean books → acquisition target |

### End State: The Calculator Platform

At $20K/mo, the business is no longer "a site with calculators." It's:
- **A content platform** (50+ calculators, 200+ blog posts, 50K+ SEO keywords)
- **A SaaS product** (freemium subscriptions, white-label, API)
- **A data business** (aggregated SaaS metrics from millions of calculations)
- **A developer platform** (API + marketplace for third-party calc authors)
- **An embed network** (500+ sites embedding WebCalc calculators)

## Exit Optionality

This business has clear exit paths at scale:

| Valuation Method | Multiple | Est. at $10K/mo | Est. at $20K/mo |
|-----------------|----------|----------------|----------------|
| Content site multiple (display ad heavy) | 25-35x monthly rev | $250-350K | $500-700K |
| SaaS multiple (subscription heavy) | 5-10x ARR | $600K-1.2M | $1.2-2.4M |
| Blended (content + SaaS) | 3-6x ARR | $360-720K | $720K-1.44M |

**Target exit**: $500K-1.5M depending on revenue mix at time of sale.

**Acquirers likely**: SaaS tool directories (G2, Capterra), SEO content aggregators (Ziff Davis, Leaf), adjacent SaaS companies looking for distribution.
