## Questioning v1.4 Assumptions

**Claim from v1.4**: "Configuration-driven generation works for all 50 calculator types."
- **SEO implication**: Programmatic generation risks thin content if every calculator page has the same template. CalculatorCove's 3,500-word pages prove content depth wins, but' 200-word pages prove it's not strictly necessary. Target 500-1,000 words per calculator with unique content per page. The config schema must include per-calculator content blocks, not just meta.

**Claim from v1.4**: "SSG for all calculator pages."
- **SEO implication**: SSG at build time is ideal. But as we scale to 200+ calculators, incremental builds (ISR) become necessary. CalculatorCove uses pure static HTML with no build pipeline (they add pages manually). Our config-driven approach is more scalable.

**Claim from v1.4**: "URL-driven state for shareability."
- **SEO implication**: Excellent for SEO. Ensure canonical URLs point to the base page (without params) to avoid duplicate content issues.

## Content Strategy

### Calculator Page Structure (500-1,000 words per page)
Informed by CalculatorCove's best-in-class 3,500-word Stripe Fee page structure:

```
1. H1: [Calculator Name]  -  Calculate [Metric] in Seconds
2. Intro paragraph (2-3 sentences defining the metric and why it matters)
3. Category badge + Verified badge with source citation (following CalculatorCove pattern)
4. The calculator widget (interactive tool)
5. How to use this calculator (numbered steps with worked example)
6. Formula box with real example (styled like + CalculatorCove)
7. Data tables with comparative benchmarks (CalculatorCove's best feature)
8. Industry benchmarks / what "good" looks like
9. Info boxes / Warning boxes for edge cases
10. Related calculators (internal links, 3-4)
11. FAQ section (5-12 questions with FAQPage schema markup  -  CalculatorCove has 12)
12. Feedback widget: "Was this helpful? Yes/No" (CalculatorCove's engagement signal)
13. CTA: "Get your free [metric] analysis" (Pro upsell)
```

## Programmatic SEO Flywheel

The calculators themselves are a **programmatic SEO engine**:

1. Build 20 calculators → index 20 unique keyword-targeting pages
2. Each page generates search traffic for its specific keyword
3. As traffic grows, domain authority increases
4. Higher DA means calculator pages rank for more long-tail queries
5. More traffic → higher RPM → more revenue
6. Revenue funds more content → more calculators → loop

**Monthly output**: 1 blog post can support 3-4 calculator pages via internal links. 8 posts/month × 4 calculators = 32 pages boosted per month.

## Key SEO Decisions (Answered by v2.1 Competitor Research)

1. **KD < 15 for a DA 0 domain.** ranks #1 for "MRR calculator" with KD ~22, but they have existing DA. For a new domain, start with KD < 10 for months 1-6.
2. **500-800 words minimum, 1,000+ for differentiation.** proves 200 words can rank #1. CalculatorCove proves 3,500 words builds authority. Target 500-800 as baseline, add depth over time.
3. **Comparison pages work  -  target tool comparisons.** "Our MRR calculator vs Baremetrics"  -  these are high-intent SaaS founder searches.
4. **2-3 posts/week minimum for months 1-6.** Competitors have zero blog content. This is our window to establish topical authority before they respond.
5. **CalculatorCove's approach is safe**: 3,500 unique words + schema per page. Our 500-800 word pages + 30-40% unique content per page + blog support cluster avoids thin content risk.
