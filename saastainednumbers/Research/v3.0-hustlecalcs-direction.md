## 1. Strategic Rationale

 (.com) has validated a massive market opportunity: **practical, specific calculators for builders and hustlers**. With 54 calculators, 1.3M calculations/month, and 182 countries served, they've proven there's demand far beyond the SaaS metrics niche.

**Why we pivot:**
- Our current 25 SaaS-only calculators serve a narrow audience
-' scope (AI costs, side hustle, personal finance, creator tools) reaches 10x the market
- Our infrastructure is already proven  -  169 passing E2E tests, 71 unit tests, multilingual (6 languages), embed API
- We have a competitive edge: **100% free forever** (they charge $9-19/mo for features)

## 7. Calculator Creation Template

Each new calculator should follow the existing conventions:

```
calculators/engine/<slug>.ts        -  Pure TS function
calculators/config/<slug>.ts        -  Config (inputs, results, FAQ, content, benchmarks)
tests/calculators/<slug>.test.ts    -  Unit tests
tests/e2e/<slug>.spec.ts           -  E2E smoke test
```

Minimum content per calculator:
- **Intro text** (2-3 sentences explaining what it does and why it matters)
- **Formula explanation** (how the calculation works)
- **8 FAQ items** (practical questions with answers)
- **Benchmark data** (industry standards or reference values)
- **Affiliate links** (at least 1 in FAQ or content via `[text](url)` syntax)
- **500+ words** total content
