Headline findings

1 · Ads alone won't pay at near-term traffic. Finance RPM ≈ $3.34 → 100k pageviews/mo is ~$90–430. A solo 300-calculator site removed ads entirely ("not life changing"). Omni Calculator's ad model needed 20M visits/mo. Treat ads as a topping, not the meal. [1][14][15]

2 · AI Overviews eat informational clicks — your exact query type. AIO on 48% of queries, >70% of informational SERPs; position-1 CTR −58% when present; non-AIO CTR still −41% YoY. Being #1 is no longer the prize — being the cited, embedded, linked-to tool is. [2][3][4][5]

3 · The embed loop is your compounding asset — and it was broken. Ungated embeds with a keyword-rich "Powered by" anchor earn DA 15–65 contextual links. Our copied embed code contained no anchor (attribution lived inside the iframe, invisible to crawlers). Fixed 2026-11-11 in lib/embed.ts. [6][7]

4 · Affiliate is the best margin per visitor today. SaaS tools pay 20–30% recurring (Baremetrics 20%, ConvertKit 30% lifetime, HubSpot 30%×12mo). A user who just computed their churn problem is the hottest lead alive. [9]

5 · Ad networks are a milestone ladder. JOURNEY by Mediavine: 1,000 tier-1 sessions/30d to enter; Mediavine full: $5,000/yr ad revenue; EthicalAds sells $5.25 tier-1 CPM directly. Roadmap: AdSense → JOURNEY → Mediavine. [10][11]

6 · Guardrail: Google's scaled-content policy (Aug 2026) demotes mass templated pages — 40–60% traffic drops observed. 87 real tools are safe; the FAQ/blog/locale templates need per-page differentiation + annual benchmark refresh. Enforce lib/content-uniqueness.ts per release. [16][7]
